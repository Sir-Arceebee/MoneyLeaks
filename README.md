## 🫠 Sound familiar?

You signed up for a free trial at 2 a.m. while studying. You meant to cancel it. Then you forgot, and now it’s $14.99 a month, every month, for something you haven’t opened since September.

Students don’t lose money on big mistakes. They lose it in small leaks that nobody notices: a trial that rolled into a paid plan, a subscription you forgot about, a student discount you never knew you qualified for. When money is tight, those leaks add up to real meals and real rent.

MoneyLeaks is a Cubbie Skill that watches your inbox so you don’t have to. It spots free trials before they start charging, finds subscriptions you’ve forgotten, and points out student discounts you’re missing. Then it texts you in plain words, in time to do something about it. No new app and no dashboard. It’s just a text that says “this trial charges you tomorrow.”

**Your money should stay yours.**

## 🔒 The promises

Money Leaks touches your inbox, so here are the rules it follows:

- 📨 **Billing emails only.** It never opens, reads or quotes your personal emails.
- 🧾 **No made-up numbers.** Every price and date comes from your emails or the company's own website. If it isn't sure, it says *"possible"* and asks you.
- 🙅 **It never cancels, logs in or pays for you.** You get the link and the steps, and you stay in control.
- 🏠 **Your data stays in your chat.** No servers and no sharing. Nothing leaves your conversation with Cubbie.
- 🎣 **Phishing aware.** Sketchy "update your card" emails get flagged, never linked.
- 🤝 **Zero judgment.** Subscriptions are fine. The goal is only to pay for what you actually use.

*Money Leaks points out charges and your options. It isn't financial advice.*

## 🧠 Handles the messy stuff

<details>
<summary><b>Yearly plans</b></summary>
Shows the real amount ("$99/yr, renews Mar 12") plus the monthly equivalent, and always warns you before it renews.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Family and shared plans</b></summary>
If you're the payer, it says so. If you're on someone else's plan, it doesn't count it. If it can't tell, it asks.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Other currencies</b></summary>
Keeps the original currency (for example "plus €9.99") and never converts it behind your back.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Already cancelled or refunded</b></summary>
Not counted. Refunds come off the total, and cancelled plans just get a note saying when access ends.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>App Store and Google Play charges</b></summary>
Splits them by app and reminds you that you cancel those in your phone's subscription settings, not on the app's website.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>No leaks found</b></summary>
Congrats, you're a financial legend 🙌. It offers to watch for new trials anyway.
</details>

## ❓ FAQ

**Is it free?** Yes. It only uses abilities Cubbie already has.

**Can it see my bank account?** No. It only reads billing emails from companies, and never your bank.

**What if it flags something I actually use?** Text "keep it" and it won't flag it again, unless the price goes up.

**How do I update it?** Text Cubbie the same link again with "update to this".

**How do I remove it?** Text "remove money-leaks".
